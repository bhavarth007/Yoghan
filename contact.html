COMMERCIAL FAQ & TERMS

Frequently Asked Purchasing Questions

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

For domestic deliveries, our standard MOQ is 1 Metric Tonne (approx. 1 truckload or combined assortment). For sea freight export, MOQ is 1x20ft FCL container.

Can we request sample bobbins for loom trial?

Yes, trial packages (1 to 2 sample bobbins) with full Uster 5 data sheet are dispatched via courier within 24 hours of commercial verification.

What quality certifications accompany each shipment?

Every consignment includes an official Certificate of Analysis (COA) specifying exact denier, tenacity, elongation, boiling water shrinkage, and OEKO-TEX Standard 100 compliance.

What are the standard payment terms?

We offer spot payment discounts, confirmed LC at sight for export, and revolving credit terms for verified high-volume contractual mills.
