CONTINUOUS VK TUBE PROCESS

Closed-Loop Synthesis & Spinning

01 Polymerization: Continuous VK column at 260°C under inert N₂ blanket.
02 Demonomerization: Hot water counter-current washing down to < 0.55% oligomers.
03 Melt Extrusion: High-speed Oerlikon Barmag spinning towers up to 5,000 m/min.
04 Optical QA: 100% online tension and package shape scanning with robotic packing.

MANUFACTURING EXCELLENCE

European & Japanese Engineering at Scale

Our Surat polymer complex pairs automated VK columns with Oerlikon Barmag WINGS take-up winders. We operate with a Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) policy, recycling 100% of caprolactam wash streams and recovering high-purity crystalline ammonium sulphate.
