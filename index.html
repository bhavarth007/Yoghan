THE YOGHAN HERITAGE

Three decades at the epicenter of India's textile capital

Established in Surat, Gujarat — the textile capital responsible for 40% of India's synthetic fabric — YOGHAN was founded with a singular ambition: to engineer polyamide yarns that match the highest European and Japanese spinning standards.

What began as a single-line texturizing plant has grown into a fully integrated chemical and synthetic fibre manufacturing complex. We operate continuous caprolactam polymerization, ultra-high-speed spinning towers, and precision texturizing bays equipped with computerized optical inspection.

INFRASTRUCTURE & PROCESS

From Caprolactam Monomer to High-Tenacity Package

A closed-loop, highly automated production process ensuring exact crystalline structure, zero tint variability, and sustainable recovery.

01

Polymerization

Continuous VK column hydrolytic polymerization of caprolactam into uniform Polyamide 6 chains with precise RV control.

VK-Tube Continuous
02

Chips Demonomerization

Hot water counter-current washing extracts residual low-molecular weight oligomers down to < 0.55%, followed by nitrogen inert drying.

Residual < 0.55%
03

Melt Spinning & Drawing

High-precision gear metering pumps and micro-hole spinnerets quench filaments under laminar cross-flow air, drawn over heated godets.

Speeds up to 5,000 m/min
04

Optical QA & Barcoded Packing

100% online tension monitoring and bobbin optical scanners check for broken filaments, package shape, and weight tolerance.

Uster Tester 5 Certified
