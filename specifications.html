Physical Testing Laboratory & Instrumentation

Our dedicated physical testing lab is maintained continuously at 20 ± 2°C and 65 ± 2% relative humidity in accordance with ISO 139 standards. Standard testing equipment includes:

Uster Tester 5 & Tension Monitors

Online and offline testing of mass variation, thin/thick places, and hairiness for high-speed yarn spinning packages.

Textechno Statimat ME+ Tensile Tester

Automated tensile testing measuring breaking force (cN), tenacity (g/denier), elongation at break (%), and modulus.

Lenzing Thermal Stress & Shrinkage Analyzer

Precision boiling water shrinkage (BWS) and hot air shrinkage (HAS) testing for flawless dyeing and heat-setting performance.

Ubbelohde Viscometry & Karl Fischer Titration

Relative viscosity testing in 96% sulfuric acid and coulometric moisture analysis down to parts-per-million accuracy.
