THE YOGHAN HERITAGE

Three Decades at the Epicenter of India's Textile Capital

Established in Surat, Gujarat — the textile capital responsible for 40% of India's synthetic fabric — YOGHAN was founded with a singular ambition: to engineer polyamide yarns that match the highest European and Japanese spinning standards.

COMMITTED TO SYNTHETIC EXCELLENCE

Pioneering Polyamide 6 Innovation Since 1994

What began as a single-line texturizing plant in Surat has grown into a fully integrated chemical and synthetic fibre manufacturing complex. We operate continuous caprolactam polymerization, ultra-high-speed spinning towers, and precision texturizing bays equipped with computerized optical inspection.

Today, YOGHAN supplies continuous filament yarns and virgin polymer chips to premium fabric manufacturers across 28 Indian states, as well as export hubs across Asia, Europe, and the Middle East.

OUR OPERATIONAL VALUES

CHRONOLOGY

Key Milestones in Our Journey

1994 — Foundation in Surat

Inception of the first texturizing unit in Surat GIDC catering to local jacquard and georgette saree weavers.

2002 — Continuous Polymerization Commissioning

Installed continuous VK column hydrolytic polymerization reactor for virgin Polyamide 6 chips production.

2011 — High-Speed European Spinning Lines

Integration of high-speed Oerlikon Barmag POY and FDY winder equipment achieving take-up speeds up to 5,000 m/min.

2018 — Agro-Grade Ammonium Sulphate Extraction

Commissioned multi-effect evaporator crystallization line recovering pure crystalline ammonium sulphate (21% N, 24% S).

2024–2026 — Line 4 Expansion (+12,000 MT/Year)

Major expansion bringing total annual spinning capacity to 48,000 MT with online robotic packaging and barcode traceability.
