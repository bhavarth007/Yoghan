STEP-BY-STEP ENGINEERING

The 4-Stage Continuous Production Journey

How we ensure tight relative viscosity, minimal oligomer residues, and flawless physical properties across every spinning lot.

01

VK-Tube Continuous Polymerization

Liquid caprolactam monomer is pumped into vertical continuous polymerization columns (VK tubes) with exact water catalyst and molecular chain terminators at 260°C under an inert nitrogen blanket.

VK-Tube Continuous
02

Hot Water Demonomerization & Drying

Polymer strands are water-quenched, pelletized, and subjected to counter-current hot water extraction to wash out unreacted caprolactam and cyclic oligomers down to < 0.55%, followed by inert nitrogen drying to < 0.06% moisture.

Extractables < 0.55%
03

Melt Spinning & Heated Godet Drawing

High-precision planetary metering gear pumps deliver molten nylon through laser-drilled micro spinnerets. Filaments pass through laminar cross-flow quench air, receive antistatic spin finish, and are oriented over heated multi-zone godet rolls.

Speeds up to 5,000 m/min
04

Optical QA & Robotic Barcode Packing

100% online optical cameras scan yarn profile, tension variation, and broken filaments before automatic bobbin doffing. Every package is barcoded with lot ID, operator stamp, tare weight, and net weight before automated vacuum shrink-wrapping.

100% Automated Scanning

PLANT ASSETS & HARDWARE

Surat Manufacturing Fleet & Equipment

Our facility operates 24/7 across multiple dedicated spinning bays, continuous polymerization reactors, and automated conditioning warehouses.

Oerlikon Barmag Spinning Towers

High-speed WINGS (Winding Integrated Guide System) winder modules providing optimized package build and smooth unwinding tension during high-speed warping.

Continuous Polymerization VK Reactors

Direct caprolactam hydrolytic polymerization columns with computerized heat zones ensuring tight polydispersity index and constant relative viscosity.

High-Speed Texturizing Bays

Polyurethane friction-disc texturizing machines with automated individual thread line tension monitoring, ensuring consistent crimp contraction and stretch stability.

Chemical Byproduct Recovery Plant

Multi-effect evaporators and vacuum crystallization centrifuge units recovering high-purity crystalline ammonium sulphate from caprolactam wash streams.
